# HealthLink

HealthLink connects Apple Health data with Home Assistant so users can understand personal baselines, create combined health-and-home sensors, compare health observations with room conditions, and build context-aware automations without writing YAML.

The easiest path uses Apple Health sensors already exposed by the official Home Assistant iOS Companion app. HealthLink discovers them automatically and keeps raw data in a local private store instead of flooding Home Assistant Recorder.

HealthLink is a wellness/context integration, not a medical device. Its personal baselines, recovery context and observed correlations are not diagnoses or medical recommendations.
